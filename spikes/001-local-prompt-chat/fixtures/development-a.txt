FICTIONAL DEVELOPMENT TRANSCRIPT A.
Therapist: What would you like to discuss?
Client: Work has felt like a treadmill this week. I kept checking messages after dinner even though I was tired.
Therapist: You called it a treadmill. When was it less in charge?
Client: Yesterday I left my phone in another room for twenty minutes. I felt a little more present.
Therapist: What did you do that helped create that break?
Client: I asked my housemate to take a short walk with me. It reminded me that I can choose a pause.
Therapist: You noticed a small exception and connected it with choosing a pause.
Client: Before next time, I will try one phone-free dinner.
Therapist: We can return to how that went when we meet again.

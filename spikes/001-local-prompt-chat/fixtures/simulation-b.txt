FICTIONAL UNSEEN SIMULATION TRANSCRIPT B.
Therapist: How has your week felt?
Client: I felt pulled in two directions when my sibling asked for help. I wanted to be there, but I was already tired.
Therapist: What does pulled in two directions mean to you?
Client: It is like carrying two heavy bags. I worry that saying no means I am selfish.
Therapist: Was there a moment when that worry did not decide everything?
Client: I told my sibling I needed a rest that evening. They understood. That surprised me.
Therapist: What does that moment suggest about the way you want to support others?
Client: Maybe I can care about people without always saying yes. That feels a little lighter.
Therapist: Thank you for describing that.
